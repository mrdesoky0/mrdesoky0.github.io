desoky

Personal Gmail integration for a local AI assistant

desoky is a private, single-user automation tool. It connects one Google Account — the owner's own Gmail account — to a locally-run AI assistant, so the owner can read, search, send, and organise their own email using natural language instead of a web interface.

What it does

  • Reads and searches messages in the owner's own mailbox
  • Sends and replies to email at the owner's direction
  • Organises mail using labels and read/unread state
  • Manages basic Gmail settings such as filters

Who uses it

This application is not distributed. It is operated by a single individual, on their own devices, against their own Google Account. There are no other users, no sign-up, and no public service. It is not offered, sold, or made available to third parties.

Data handling

Data accessed through Google APIs stays on the owner's own machines. Nothing is sent to any third-party server, analytics service, or advertiser. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
